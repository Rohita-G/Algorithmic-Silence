## Layer B: Source-Based Algorithmic & Systematic Inspection

Direct inspection of `data/raw/hamlet.txt` against extracted tables in `validation/`:

1. **Stage Direction Isolation Audit (`validation/removed_spans_audit.csv`)**:
   - Exactly **266 bracketed stage directions** (1,060 words) identified and excluded.
   - All 20 multi-line stage direction blocks (totaling 342 words) verified completely removed.
   - Dumb Show in Act 3 Scene 2 (lines 2986–2997, 119 words) verified excluded; 0 words leaked into Hamlet's speech.
   - Front matter (lines 1–43, 159 words) verified excluded.
   - Act and scene headings (25 headings, 50 words) verified excluded.
   - Speaker labels (1,132 turn prefix tags, ~1,173 words) verified excluded from dialogue word counts.
   - **Parenthetical Verse Inspection:** All 41 parenthetical spans `(...)` in `data/raw/hamlet.txt` verified as legitimate spoken dialogue and 100% preserved. Zero spoken dialogue words enclosed in square brackets.

2. **Line 2744 Speaker Misattribution Resolution**:
   - In raw text: `KING, [advancing with Polonius]` (line 2744).
   - Patched regex handles trailing commas before brackets. Claudius's 108-word monologue (`Love? His affections do not that way tend...`) is correctly assigned to King Claudius (Turn 485).
   - Ophelia's speech turn (Turn 484) ends cleanly at line 2720 (`O heavenly powers, restore him!`), establishing Ophelia's verified clean total of **1,180 words across 58 turns**.

3. **Honorific Address & Addressee Audit (`validation/presence_addressee_annotations.csv`)**:
   - Generated by heuristic mapping rules in `scripts/build_addressee_audit.py` and reviewed by a single researcher.
   - Exact phrase `"my lord"` appears **27 times** across **26 discrete turns** (44.8% turn prevalence).
   - Lexical variant `"What means your Lordship?"` appears in Turn 475 (bringing variant-inclusive total to 28).
   - **Addressee Breakdown:** 18 occurrences addressed to Prince Hamlet (Act 3 Scene 1 & 2); 9 occurrences addressed to Lord Polonius (Act 1 Scene 3 & Act 2 Scene 1). Zero occurrences addressed to any social equal or subordinate.
   - *Provenance Note:* This mapping is an automated heuristic baseline audited by a single researcher, not an independent multi-reader human ground truth.

4. **Network Edge Transition Audit (`validation/ophelia_adjacency_audit.csv`)**:
   - Generated directly from raw text dialogue extraction via `scripts/build_source_audits.py`.
   - All **115 conversational turn transitions** involving Ophelia traced to exact turn IDs and line ranges:
     - Hamlet: 54 transitions
     - Polonius: 22 transitions
     - Laertes: 18 transitions
     - Queen Gertrude: 10 transitions
     - King Claudius: 10 transitions
     - Prologue Player: 1 transition

## Layer C: Independent Human Validation Protocol & Limitation

> **Transparent Scientific Limitation Statement:**  
> The automated checks (Layer A) and algorithmic line inspections (Layer B) verify internal consistency and code execution. However, **independent human validation** requires an external human scholar to independently review extracted dialogue turns against the raw text without reliance on the automated parser. No external human double-blind annotation has been completed for this dataset.

2. Check that no words from preceding or succeeding stage directions are included in dialogue.
3. Verify that speaker tags were not counted in words.
4. Sign with date and initials once verified.
